Everything businesses ask us before booking a virtual tour.

How much does a 360° virtual tour cost in Pakistan?

We charge PKR 8,000–10,000 per viewpoint, depending on the space. Publishing the tour on your Google Business Profile is PKR 20,000. A small café usually needs around 6 viewpoints, a restaurant around 12, and a banquet hall or hotel around 20. Interactive features like hotspots, video, booking links and branded skins are quoted per project.

Do you work outside Karachi?

Yes. We are based in Karachi and take projects across Pakistan, including Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Travel for out-of-city shoots is quoted with your project.

What is a viewpoint?

A viewpoint is one spot the 360° camera shoots from. Each viewpoint becomes one scene in your tour, and visitors move from scene to scene to walk through your space.

Will my virtual tour show on Google Maps?

Yes. With the Google Business Profile upload, your tour is published to your listing so people can walk through your space from Google Maps and Google Search, the same way they use Street View.

How long does it take to make a virtual tour?

Most spaces are shot in a single visit, and the finished tour is usually ready within the week.

Do I have to close my business for the shoot?

No. The shoot is planned around your schedule so there is no disruption to your business.

Can I put the tour on my website and share it on WhatsApp?

Yes. Every tour can be embedded on your website and shared as a link on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere else. It works on phones and desktops without any app.

Which businesses use virtual tours?

Restaurants and cafés, banquet and wedding halls, coworking spaces and offices, gyms, hotels and guest houses, showrooms, clinics, salons and real estate. Any business where customers choose you by how your space looks and feels.
