What businesses usually ask before booking.

How much does a 360° virtual tour cost in Pakistan?

We charge PKR 8,000–10,000 per viewpoint, depending on the space. Publishing the tour on your Google Business Profile is PKR 20,000. Interactive features like hotspots, custom video, booking links and branded skins are quoted per project.

What is a viewpoint?

A viewpoint is one spot the 360° camera shoots from. Each viewpoint becomes one scene in your tour, and visitors move from scene to scene to walk through your space.

How many viewpoints will my space need?

As a rough guide, a small café needs around 6, a restaurant around 12, a banquet hall or hotel around 20, and a large office or campus 30 or more. We confirm the exact number after a quick look at your location.

Is the Google Business Profile upload required?

No, it's optional. Without it you still get a tour you can put on your website and share as a link. With it, your tour also appears on your Google Maps and Search listing.

Do you work outside Karachi?

Yes. We are based in Karachi and take projects across Pakistan, including Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Travel for out-of-city shoots is quoted with your project.
